Artículo de Periódico

A COMPANY BOSS AT 16

When David Eisman was just 16 and still at high school, he set up his own video game company. He always wanted to work in the video game industry.

He filled in applications for different companies to do an internship but they turned him down because they said he had no experience. He kept at it but always faced the same problem. That didn't put him off, though.

He decided to do something radical. He couldn't take over an existing company because he didn't have any money. So he started his own company by getting people to work for him for free, by offering to share the company's profits with them. He and his team began to work on an educational game.

Being the boss of a company is hard work and it's difficult to keep up with all the elements of game production. But if you want to get ahead in the world of work, don't put things off until tomorrow if you can do them today!
